🧹 About Text Cleaner

A free, privacy-first online text formatting tool

Text Cleaner is a lightweight web tool designed to help you quickly clean and format text. Whether you're dealing with messy copy from PDFs, emails, or transcripts, Text Cleaner simplifies the process with just a few clicks.

✨ What We Offer

  • Remove leading/trailing spaces
  • Collapse or remove all spaces
  • Delete empty lines and unify line breaks
  • Remove special characters (with customizable whitelist)
  • Case conversion (UPPER, lower, Capitalize)
  • Preserve audio transcript format (speaker + timestamp)

🔒 Privacy First

All processing happens entirely in your browser. We never upload, store, or transmit your text to any server. Your data stays on your device, always.
