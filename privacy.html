🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Text Cleaner (accessible at ssdatastream.com) is a free online text formatting tool. Your privacy is important to us.

1. Data Processing

All text processing is performed entirely in your browser. We do not upload, store, or transmit any text you enter to our servers or any third-party services. Your data never leaves your device.

2. No Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information or text content you input into the tool. There is no registration, no cookies for tracking, and no analytics that collect personal data.

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on this website. Google AdSense may use cookies and web beacons to show relevant ads. These cookies do not collect personal information. You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at Google Privacy Policy.

Our website may contain links to external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.
